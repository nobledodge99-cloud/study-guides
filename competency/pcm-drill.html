PCM Competency Drill

OCS III · 10 minutes · chief complaint → focused exam → special tests → assessment → plan. Findings are hidden until you "perform" them, just like the findings cards on exam day.

How it's gradedPractice a caseStudy sheets (all 12)Say-it templates

What the station wants

From the course overview and the faculty email.

You get
  • A chief complaint + supporting clinical information (vitals, short history). No list of exams.
  • A findings card for every system exam or special test you choose to perform.
You do (10 minutes)
  1. Select and perform a focused, systematic exam, verbalizing as you go
  2. Indicated special tests, then interpret
  3. Brief assessment
  4. Prioritized plan
Rules that cost points
  • If you don't say it, it didn't happen. Verbalize every maneuver.
  • Don't perform everything. Choose tests that answer a question and say what that question is.
  • Assessment is synthesis, not a list: leading dx + supporting findings + why it beats the alternatives + alternatives and why they're less likely.
  • Plan: workup, treatment/medication, patient education, follow-up, return precautions. Prioritize and give the rationale.

Say-it templates

Opening

"Hi, I'm Noble, one of the medical students. May I examine you today? [Hand hygiene.] I understand you're here for ___. Based on that, my main considerations are ___, ___, and ___, so I'll examine the ___ and ___ systems."

While examining

"I'm inspecting for ___. I'm palpating the ___ for ___. I'm testing ___ to rule in or out ___."

After each card

"This finding supports ___ and makes ___ less likely, so next I'll ___."

Assessment

"My leading diagnosis is ___. Supporting this: ___, ___, and ___. I favor it over ___ because ___. Less likely are ___ because ___ and ___ because ___."

Plan

"First, ___ (workup/disposition). For treatment, ___. I would educate on ___. Follow up in ___. Return or go to the ED for ___."

Every plan includes

  • Workup/imaging/labs with the reason
  • Treatment or medication (name + class + one safety point)
  • Patient education
  • Follow-up interval
  • Return precautions / safety net
