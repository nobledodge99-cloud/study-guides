Diuretics · Dr. Waterfall

When to reach for this section: the vignette gives a nephron site, an electrolyte pattern, or a clinical scenario (pulmonary edema, ascites, glaucoma, high ICP, hypercalcemia, recurrent calcium stones, diabetes insipidus, SIADH-type hyponatremia, Liddle syndrome).

Thiazide & Thiazide-Like Diuretics

β-Blockers

β-blocker class: ↓ HR, contractility, AV conduction, renin; abrupt withdrawal → rebound angina, MI, hypertensive crisis (taper). Contraindicated: severe bradycardia, high-degree AV block, decompensated HF; nonselective agents in asthma/COPD; caution diabetes (masks hypoglycemia), PVD. Verapamil/diltiazem → additive bradycardia/AV block; NSAIDs blunt effect.

All arteriolar dilators cause reflex tachycardia and Na⁺/water retention: add a β-blocker and a diuretic (loop diuretic with minoxidil). Site rule: venous dilation ↓ preload; arteriolar ↓ afterload; nitroprusside balanced.

Hyperkalemia: Stabilize → Shift → Remove

Calcium stabilizes the myocardium; insulin and β₂-agonists shift K⁺ into cells; diuretics, binders and dialysis remove K⁺. Hyperkalemia should not automatically stop ACEI/ARBs: lower K⁺ first.
