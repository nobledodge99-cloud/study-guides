Mission Statement

Our mission statement is to audaciously live and lead by example. To utilize the unique giftings God has granted us to lead, influence, and encourage each other and the watching world towards God’s divine will and way. We resolve to hold one another accountable, encourage growth and development, and challenge each other to be attentive and responsive to God’s calling in each other’s unique way. We aim to engage in fellowship in order to embolden ourselves and each other to more effectively go forth as credible witnesses and embody what a man of God is.
