How this was built. Every table in every guide was extracted automatically, rows for the same drug or disease were merged, and the columns were mapped by header (mechanism, uses, side effects, pearl). Each fact keeps a link to the guide it came from.

Limits: column mapping is by header wording, so an odd table may put a fact under the wrong heading. Check the source guide for anything high yield. Renal pharmacology is not here yet, since there is no guide for it. Abbreviations are spelled out at the first place they appear in the default A–Z order only; once you sort, filter or search, use the key below.
