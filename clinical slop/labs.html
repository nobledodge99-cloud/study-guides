1 · The basics

How to read itSort the liver panel into three buckets: hepatocellular injury (AST/ALT), cholestasis (ALP/GGT/direct bili), synthetic function (albumin/INR/bili). Then use the pattern and the magnitude. R factor = (ALT ÷ ULN) ÷ (ALP ÷ ULN): >5 hepatocellular, 2–5 mixed, <2 cholestatic.

PT / INR

1 · The basics

TestReferencePathway / factorsUsed for
