KINDER — RESPIRATORY ONE-PAGER

COPD/Asthma · CF/Bronchiectasis · ILD/Sarcoid/Pneumoconiosis · Imaging · ABG

COPD

  • Progressive, mostly irreversible obstruction; FEV1/FVC <70%. Smoking = #1. A1AT def = 1–2% (young, panacinar, lower lobe + liver dz/cirrhosis).
  • Centriacinar = smoking, upper lobe. Panacinar = A1AT, lower lobe. Cause: elastase/antielastase imbalance.
  • Pink puffer = emphysema (thin, pursed-lip). Blue bloater = chronic bronchitis (cyanotic, edema/cor pulmonale).
  • ↓ elastic recoil, ↑ airway resistance (airways <2 mm). Hypercapnia only in severe.
  • Chronic CO₂ retainers rely on hypoxic drive → high-flow O₂ → hypoventilation (+ V/Q worsens, Haldane). Target SpO₂ 88–92%.
  • GOLD (all FEV1/FVC <70%): I ≥80% · II 50–79 · III 30–49 · IV <30 (or <50 + resp failure).
  • CXR: hyperinflation, flat diaphragm, ↑ retrosternal space, bullae (can be normal early).
  • Only 2 mortality-reducing tx: smoking cessation + long-term O₂ (if chronic hypoxemia). Rest = symptoms/exacerbations. LAMA tiotropium > LABA; roflumilast (PDE4); theophylline (narrow window). LVRS = upper-lobe emphysema.
  • Exacerbation: H. flu, S. pneumo, Moraxella (Pseudomonas if severe/recent hosp). Tx: antibiotics (purulent sputum), systemic steroids, SABA, O₂ >90%, NIPPV.

ASTHMA

  • 3 parts: reversible obstruction + hyper-responsiveness + inflammation. Boys > girls pre-puberty; women > men after.
  • ACh → M3 = main constrictor (histamine minor). NO ↑ in attack (FeNO).
  • Samter's triad: asthma + aspirin/NSAID sensitivity + nasal polyps (leukotriene shunt).
  • Exam: polyphonic expiratory wheeze, pulsus paradoxus. Quiet chest = severe. Ominous: can't speak, drowsy, cyanosis.
  • ABG: low PaCO₂ is normal → "normal" PaCO₂ = tiring, impending failure.
  • Labs: eosinophilia, ↑ IgE (COPD = neutrophils). CXR often normal; PTX/pneumomediastinum in severe. EKG: sinus tach, RAD, RBBB, P pulmonale.
  • Categories: mild intermittent ≤2 d/wk, ≤2 nights/mo · mild persistent >2 d/wk · moderate persistent daily · severe persistent continual.
  • Tx: as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol now preferred reliever (not SABA alone). Step 3 low ICS-LABA → 4 medium → 5 high + biologics (anti-IgE, IL-5, IL-4R) ± tiotropium.

CYSTIC FIBROSIS

  • AR, CFTR Cl⁻ channel. ↓ Cl⁻ secretion → thick dehydrated mucus. ΔF508 (most common) = never reaches surface; G551D = at surface, won't open. Most common in White (1/2,500). Median survival ~37 y; lung dz = 80% of deaths.
  • Dx: IRT screen → genetics → sweat Cl⁻ (pilocarpine iontophoresis). 5% dx after age 18.
  • Pancreas: 85% exocrine insufficiency (greasy stools, ADEK malabsorption); ~1/3 diabetes by 30. 15% meconium ileus. Males sterile (vas deferens). Sinus polyps, liver/varices, osteopenia, nephrolithiasis.
  • PFT obstructive; CXR hyperinflation, upper-lobe bronchiectasis.
  • Pseudomonas = main pathogen (also S. aureus/MRSA, NTM, ABPA).
  • Tx: azithromycin MWF (≥6 y + Pseudomonas); inhaled tobramycin/aztreonam 28-day cycles; dornase alfa, hypertonic saline, chest PT, pancreatic enzymes, CFTR modulators; transplant.

BRONCHIECTASIS

  • Permanent dilation of bronchi from infection/inflammation cycles. ~½ due to CF, ~⅓ unknown → sweat test in every workup.
  • Chronic purulent cough, hemoptysis. Slow decline, faster with Pseudomonas.
  • HRCT dx: no tapering, bronchi in outer 1 cm, bronchus > adjacent artery (signet ring).
  • Lobe clue: upper = CF · lower = aspiration · RML/lingula = MAI · central = ABPA.
  • Other causes: PCD (EM of cilia), A1AT, hypogammaglobulinemia, HIV, RA, Sjögren's, TB/pertussis, obstruction.
  • Tx: treat cause → antimicrobials → anti-inflammatory (macrolides, ICS) → clear secretions → surgery (localized) → transplant.

ILD — BY BUCKET

Exposure

  • HP: immune-mediated; farmer's (thermophilic actinomycetes), bird fancier's, AC lung. Steroids help symptoms only.
  • Byssinosis (cotton/flax/hemp): not immune; worse Monday.
  • Drugs: amiodarone (>400 mg/d), nitrofurantoin, methotrexate.

Connective tissue

  • SSc: Scl-70, pulm HTN · RA: ⅓ ILD, low-glucose effusion, nodules · MCTD: anti-RNP

Idiopathic (IIPs)

  • IPF: exclusion dx; ↓DLCO, honeycomb/basal/subpleural, clubbing; pirfenidone/nintedanib slow FVC loss.
  • NSIP: bibasilar GGO. COP: fever, migratory infiltrates, Masson bodies, fails antibiotics → steroids.
  • RB-ILD / DIP: smokers (DIP macrophages). AIP: ARDS-like, 60% die 6 mo, not smoking. LIP: EBV/HIV.

ILD — MISC / SARCOID / PNEUMOCONIOSIS

Sarcoid

  • Non-caseating granulomas; Black > White 3.5×; ages 40–60 (women). Hilar nodes on CXR. Löfgren = erythema nodosum + arthritis + bilateral hilar nodes (good prognosis, no bx needed).
  • CXR stage: 0 normal · 1 nodes · 2 nodes + infiltrates · 3 infiltrates only · 4 fibrosis.
  • Restrictive, ↓DLCO. ↑Ca (vit D), ACE nonspecific, BAL ↑CD4/CD8. Bx safest site; transbronchial ≥85%. EN biopsy useless. Cardiac = main cause of death. Tx only if symptomatic: prednisone 20–40 mg.

Other

  • GPA: c-ANCA, sinus + lung + kidney. Goodpasture: anti-GBM, no nasal. EGPA: asthma + eos; unmasked after steroid wean/LTRA.
  • ABPA: ↑↑IgE, central bronchiectasis → steroids + itraconazole.
  • PLCH: young male smoker, cysts upper lobes, PTX, DI. LAM: women, chylous effusion. PAP: crazy-paving → whole lung lavage. Hemosiderosis: ↑DLCO.

IMAGING — HOW TO READ + PATTERNS

Approach & 3 buckets

  • ABCDEF: Airway · Bones/breast · Cardiac/mediastinum · Diaphragm/upper abd · Effusions/edges · Fields (lungs) · + devices. Review bones on every film.
  • Airspace (consolidation): fluffy, "airbrush," obscures vessels, air bronchograms. Causes: pus, water, blood, cells, aspirate.
  • Interstitial: honeycomb (UIP) · linear (edema, viral) · reticular (edema, fibrosis, silicosis) · nodular/miliary (TB, fungal, mets, sarcoid, pneumoconiosis).
  • Pleural: effusion = meniscus/blunted costophrenic angle (erect film). Pneumothorax = sharp visceral pleural line + no vascular markings beyond it.

CHF vs pneumonia (both → consolidation ± effusion)

  • CHF clues: wide vascular pedicle, cephalization (upper-lobe artery > bronchus), big heart, azygos, Kerley B, bilateral perihilar fluff. Pneumonia: normal heart + pedicle, no Kerley B, unilateral/lobar.
  • Stage 1 redistribution (PCWP 13–18) → Stage 2 interstitial: Kerley B (1–2 cm, horizontal, peripheral near CP angle = septal), peribronchial cuffing, perihilar haze (18–25) → Stage 3 alveolar: consolidation, air bronchograms, "cottonwool," effusion (>25). After diuresis, edema clears first; heart size/pedicle lag.

Pneumonia patterns

  • Typical bacterial: dense lobar. Mycoplasma: bilateral symmetric, apex-sparing; CT peribronchovascular GGO + wall thickening. Legionella: basilar → rapid multilobar spread despite tx; water-source exposure (cooling towers, ducts). Pseudomonas / S. aureus / Klebsiella / anaerobes: cavitation. COVID: peripheral bilateral GGO; US "waterfall" B-lines.

COPD / sarcoid / PTX / trauma / lines

  • COPD: >10 posterior ribs, flat diaphragm, ↑ retrosternal air (lateral).
  • Sarcoid: 1 nodes → 2 nodes + infiltrates → 3 infiltrates only → 4 fibrosis (upper-lobe volume loss, calcified).
  • PTX: small = apical, subtle → expiratory film makes it more obvious (air volume fixed, lung smaller). Tension = tracheal/mediastinal shift away + hypotension, absent breath sounds → clinical dx, needle decompression/chest tube without waiting for film. Tall thin young male = primary spontaneous PTX.
  • Subcutaneous emphysema + rib fractures → look for PTX/hemothorax/laceration. Lateral film: check for vertebral compression fx.
  • Lines: central line tip near cavoatrial junction; ETT above carina; NG/OG below diaphragm in stomach.

Nodules & screening

  • CXR nodule → CT for size, margin, density. Calcification/fat = benign; spiculated, cavitating, part-solid/GGO = worrisome.
  • LDCT annual screening: age range + heavy pack-year history + current smoker or quit <15 y. Stop at 15 y quit or limited life expectancy / not a surgical candidate.

ABG — STEP BY STEP

Hypoxemia: A-a gradient

  • Raised A-a (O₂ reaches alveoli, fails to cross): V/Q mismatch (pneumonia), R→L shunt, diffusion defect, low mixed-venous O₂. Normal A-a: hypoventilation, high altitude (low FiO₂).
  • Hypoxic + normal gradient = ventilation/FiO₂ problem; hypoxic + high gradient = lung problem.
  • PAO₂ = (Patm−47)×FiO₂ − PaCO₂/RQ (RQ 0.8; sea level room air = 100). Patm: 760 sea level, ~676 Billings, ~623 Denver.
  • Bedside: A-a = 7×FiO₂% − 1.25×PaCO₂ − PaO₂. Normal (age+10)/4, never >30. Always need FiO₂ (nasal cannula: 21 + 3 + 4/L; 2 L=28%, 4 L=36%, 6 L=44%).
  • DO₂ = CO × 1.34 × Hgb × SaO₂ → normal PaO₂ ≠ good delivery (shock, anemia).

Acid-base basics

  • Normal pH 7.40 (7.36–7.44), PaCO₂ 40, HCO₃ 24. Henderson-Hasselbalch: pH = 6.1 + log(HCO₃/0.03·PaCO₂). Minute vent = RR × TV.
  • Resp: pH and CO₂ move opposite. Metabolic: pH and HCO₃ move the same way.
  • Resp acidosis (hypoventilation): drugs, coma, stroke, NM disease, kyphoscoliosis, airway obstruction, COPD, OSA/obesity. Resp alkalosis: anxiety, asthma, PNA, PE, CHF, sepsis, fever, ↑ICP, salicylates, liver dz, over-ventilation.
  • Met acidosis: high AG = MUDPILES; normal AG (hyperchloremic) = HCO₃ loss (RTA, diarrhea). AG = Na−Cl−HCO₃ (12); low albumin lowers AG (masks). Base excess low in met acidosis, high in met alkalosis.
  • Compensation speed: buffers min · lungs hrs · kidneys 2–3 d.

6-step read

  1. pH: acidemia/alkalemia
  2. HCO₃ vs PaCO₂: metabolic or respiratory primary
  3. Uncompensated / partial / full (acute vs chronic)
  4. AG; if high → delta ratio (AG−12)/(24−HCO₃): <1 + non-gap acidosis · 1–2 pure · >2 + met alkalosis
  5. Met disorder? check PaCO₂ vs Winter's for extra resp process
  6. Does it fit the patient?

ABG — LECTURE CASES (PATTERN TO KNOW)

  • Post-hip PE: pH 7.48, CO₂ 32, HCO₃ 22, PaO₂ 95 on 60% → A-a ≈ 285. Acute resp alkalosis (hyperventilation rules out oversedation), normal CXR (no PNA/CHF) → PE → CTPA, anticoagulate. = hypoxic failure. Missing FiO₂ = can't calc A-a.
  • COPD exacerbation: pH 7.22, CO₂ 90, HCO₃ 38, PaO₂ 55 on 2 L (28%) → A-a ≈ 28.5 normal. Pure hypoventilation = hypercapnic failure.
  • Pneumonia/sepsis: pH 7.45, CO₂ 25, HCO₃ 16, PaO₂ 155 on 100% → A-a ≈ 514. Resp alkalosis + high-AG (21; lactic), delta 1.13 = pure. Repeat (pH 7.25, CO₂ 50): muscle fatigue → combined hypoxic + hypercapnic.
  • Intubated COPD: pH 7.55, CO₂ 46, HCO₃ 36 → looks like met alkalosis, but baseline is chronic resp acidosis with renal compensation; ventilator blew off CO₂ faster than kidneys adjust → acute resp alkalosis on chronic resp acidosis. Use clinical context.
